Rate limits and input caps on the AI routes. `/search`, `/search/voice`, `/search/image` and `/tts` share a 30 requests/minute per-client limit, and `/search/suggestions` has its own 120/minute limit (`aiRateLimit` on `createExpressRouter` and on the new `createNextHandlers(config, options)` second argument; `false` disables both). `suggestionsRateLimit` sets the suggestions limit on its own, because each suggestions request can make an AI call; `aiRateLimit: { limit }` does not change it. Login is also limited per email address (SHA-256 hashed): 10 failed logins in 15 minutes lock that email until the window ends, while successful logins do not count. Voice summaries (`ttsText`) are truncated in core to the new `MAX_TTS_CHARS` (500), at a sentence or word boundary, so they always pass the `/tts` cap. When a built-in limiter (Next.js, and the per-email login limiter) tracks 10,000 active keys, a new key is admitted and the lowest-count window is dropped, so floods of new keys neither lock clients out nor reset busy ones. Limited requests return 429 `RATE_LIMITED` with `Retry-After` in both adapters.

Search `query`/`refineQuery` and suggestions `query` are capped at 300 characters and `/tts` text at 500 (400 `VALIDATION`); search `limit` is clamped to 1-100; `searchTerms` (at most 20 non-empty strings of at most 300 characters), `sort` and `suggestedFacets` must have the documented shape (400 `VALIDATION` otherwise, not a 500). JSON POST routes return 415 `UNSUPPORTED_MEDIA_TYPE` unless `Content-Type` is `application/json`. Cart quantities must be integers in 1-99 (0-99 for update-quantity).

Express: the router no longer overrides express-rate-limit's key generator or disables its `X-Forwarded-For` validation, so behind a proxy set `app.set("trust proxy", n)`; hosts may pass `keyGenerator`. Next.js: rate-limit state is now per `createNextHandlers()` call instead of module-wide, the default client key is the right-most `X-Forwarded-For` entry (was the left-most, which clients can forge), and hosts may pass `clientKey`. Core: `missions.enabled: false` is now a hard opt-out that a request's `enableMissions: true` cannot override.
