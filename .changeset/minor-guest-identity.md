Guest identity and order inputs. Login sends only the ownership-checked `anonymousCart` to commercetools and never the guest `anonymousId`, which would have assigned every Cart, Order and Payment with that id to the signing-in customer. New `ANONYMOUS_ID_HEADER` (`x-commerce-ai-anonymous-id`, exported from `@commerce-ai-tool/core/client` and `@commerce-ai-tool/server`): `GET /cart`, `/cart/shipping-methods`, `/cart/payment-methods` and `/orders` read the guest id from the header; the `anonymousId` query parameter is no longer read (see the 3.0.0 migration guide). `GET /orders` with a customer session ignores the client `anonymousId`. Guest orders placed before sign-in are no longer reassigned to the customer, and `GET /orders` with a session no longer returns them.

`orderNumber` on `POST /cart/order` and `POST /cart/payment` must match `ORDER_NUMBER_PATTERN` (`cat-` plus a lowercase 8-4-4-4-12 UUID, 400 `VALIDATION` otherwise), and `createPaymentKey` sanitises it like the other key parts. New optional `CommerceAIConfig.cartPolicy` (`allowedCountries`, `currencyByCountry`) keeps the cart currency and country under server control; unset keeps today's behaviour. Checkout now sets the cart country to the shipping address country (`setCartAddresses`, re-applied in `authorizePayment` and `createOrder`), and rejects shipping countries outside `cartPolicy.allowedCountries` or whose `cartPolicy.currencyByCountry` currency differs from the cart currency. `POST /cart/addresses`, `POST /cart/payment` and `POST /cart/order` can therefore return 400 `MISSING_PRICE` when the shipping country has no price, or 400 `VALIDATION` for a disallowed country or mismatched policy currency. New `createRandomUuid` (Web Crypto, with a `getRandomValues` fallback; in `@commerce-ai-tool/core/client` and `/internal`), `CountryNotAllowedError` and `CurrencyMismatchError`; the internal `createPaymentOperations` takes optional `ShippingScopeOptions` as a third argument.
